Who We Are

Family – Run.
Louisiana – Born.

The Francis Smokehouse is a family-owned and operated BBQ and Cajun restaurant sitting on US Highway 61 in St. Francisville, Louisiana. Since opening our doors in 2015, we have been committed to one thing: cooking real food the right way.

That means hardwood-smoked meats, house-made sides, and Cajun specialties rooted in the traditions of the Louisiana bayou. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just honest food cooked with care every single day.

The Method

Low & Slow.
Every Time.

Every rack of ribs, every brisket, every pulled pork shoulder goes into the pit the same way — seasoned, loaded, and left alone. We use real hardwood and real patience. There is no other way to do it right.

Beyond the BBQ, our kitchen reaches deep into Louisiana Cajun tradition — boudin links, boudin balls, crawfish pies, and meat pies made the way they are supposed to be made. The bayou is in every bite.

Our History

How We Got Here

2015
The First Fire

The Francis Smokehouse opens on Highway 61 in St. Francisville. The pit fires up for the first time and the community starts taking notice.

Early Years
Becoming a Staple

Locals make it their weekly spot. The corn pudding becomes legendary. Word spreads. The menu grows to include po-boys, Cajun specialties, and rotating chef specials.

Recognition
Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice

Visitors from across the country start making the drive to St. Francisville specifically for the food. The Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice award follows.

Today
Still at It

The pit runs every day. 40+ items on the menu. The family is still here, still cooking, still doing it the right way.
