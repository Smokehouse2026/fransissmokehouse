Our Story
01
How It Started

Born on Highway 61.

The Francis Smokehouse opened its doors in 2015 on US Highway 61 in St. Francisville, Louisiana — a stretch of road that's been feeding travelers and locals since long before us. We started with a simple belief: that the best BBQ comes from doing one thing and doing it right.

Post oak and hickory. 225°F. A minimum of twelve hours. Every single day. The fire starts at 4am. The first plate doesn't leave until the meat is ready — not because a clock says so, but because it is.

You'll smell us before you see us.

— A standing promise to every traveler on the Great River Road
02
The Craft

No Gas. No Shortcuts.

Everything on the menu spent serious time over a real wood fire. Briskets get twelve hours minimum. Ribs are rubbed and left to develop bark and smoke ring the old-fashioned way. The pulled pork shoulder smokes until it falls apart — not when we need it to, but when it's ready.

03
Louisiana Roots

Cajun at the Core.

We're a smokehouse, but we're also Louisiana through and through. The deli case runs house-made boudin links, boudin balls, pepper jack boudin, crawfish pies, and meat pies — real Cajun comfort made from scratch every day. The sides are made fresh every morning: corn pudding, baked beans, potato salad, slaw, and sweet potato waffle fries.

And the daily specials board changes every morning at 6am — eggplant pirogue, blackened fish, BBQ shrimp, grilled salmon with crabmeat. The kind of cooking that reminds you where you are.
