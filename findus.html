Directions

Getting Here.

From Baton Rouge
Take US-61 North (Scenic Highway / Airline Hwy) through Zachary and into St. Francisville. We're on the right side of US-61 as you enter town.
~35 miles · approx. 45 min
From Natchez, MS
Head south on US-61 (the Great River Road) through Woodville into Louisiana. Continue south through Centerville — we're on the left on US-61 in St. Francisville.
~75 miles · approx. 1 hr 20 min
From New Orleans
Take I-10 West to I-110 North to US-61 North. Follow US-61 through Baton Rouge and continue north to St. Francisville. We're on US-61 on the way into town.
~100 miles · approx. 1 hr 45 min
From Jackson, MS
Take I-55 South to the Natchez Trace Parkway, or US-61 South through Natchez into Louisiana. Continue south on US-61 to St. Francisville.
~130 miles · approx. 2 hrs
When You Arrive

Look for The Smoke.

On most days when the smoker is running you'll be able to smell us from down the road. Look for the sign on the right (northbound) or left (southbound) side of US Highway 61.
🌳
Look for the outdoor deck built around the old tree — it's visible from the road.
🚗
Free parking lot directly off US-61. Pull in off the highway.
♿
Fully wheelchair accessible — both the building and the outdoor deck.
🔥
If the smoker is loaded, you'll smell post oak and hickory from down the road. That's us.
📍
We sit right on the Great River Road — a mandatory stop for anyone driving the route.
