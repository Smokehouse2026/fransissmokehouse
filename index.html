THE SMOKEHOUSE

Deep in St. Francisville, The Francis Smokehouse (aka The Francis Smokehouse & Specialty Meats) has been serving up Louisiana’s finest smoked meats since 2015. Locals pack the deck for pepper jack boudin balls, and travelers detour off Hwy 61 for brisket that “practically melts.”

Our pit masters honor slow‑smoked traditions while spinning out inventive daily specials: Jack Daniel’s BBQ shrimp, blackened lemon fish, and homemade bread pudding. It’s rustic, cozy, and always welcoming – even for four‑legged friends on the outdoor deck.

2010Hill Country Smokehouse opens (Commerce St.) – still a local legend.
2015The Francis Smokehouse opens on Hwy 61; “The Francis Southern Table & Bar” launches same year.
2025Travellers’ Choice & 4.7 stars – still family‑run, still smoking.
