Where It Started

Highway 61
Runs Through Everything

When the doors opened in 2015, the mission was simple: cook honest food the way it was meant to be cooked. No shortcuts. Real wood. Real smoke. Real patience. What started as a passion for Louisiana pit BBQ grew into something the community claimed as their own.

The Method

Low & Slow
Is the Only Way

Every rack of ribs, every brisket, every pulled pork shoulder starts the same way — seasoned, loaded into the pit, and left alone. Real smoke takes time. Beyond the BBQ, the kitchen extends into the Louisiana Cajun tradition — fresh boudin, crawfish pies, and egg rolls that bring the bayou straight to the table.

The Journey

Milestones

2015
The Doors Open

The Francis Smokehouse fires up the pit for the first time on Highway 61 in St. Francisville.

Early Years
A Community Staple

Locals start returning weekly. The corn pudding becomes legendary. The menu grows to include po-boys, Cajun specialties, and rotating chef specials.

Recognition
Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice

The smokehouse earns a Tripadvisor Travellers’ Choice award. Visitors from across the country start making the drive.

Today
Still Burning

The pit runs every day. 40+ items on the menu. The family is still at the center of everything.
