OUR LEGACY

Crafted by fire & family

For over a decade, Bayou Smokehouse has been a gathering place where friends and families come together over honest, wood‑smoked barbecue. Our pit masters start before sunrise, tending oak and pecan fires that burn low and slow, infusing every brisket, rib, and sausage with deep, rich flavor.

We source locally, smoke daily, and serve with a side of genuine Louisiana warmth. Come as you are, leave happy – that’s the Smokehouse way.
