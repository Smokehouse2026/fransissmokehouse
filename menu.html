Why this feels different

Most restaurant menus are rigid and expected. This one behaves like a sensory object: floating flavor nodes, atmospheric motion, responsive depth, search-driven discovery, cinematic cards, and a premium, almost sci-fi mood without losing the Southern smokehouse soul.

It is weird in the right way. Distinctive. Memorable. Hard to confuse with anything else.
