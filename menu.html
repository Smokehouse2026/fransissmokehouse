Brand signals

What the public web says this place feels like

The site design leans into the smokehouse being described publicly as a Southern comfort destination serving barbecue plates, burgers, po-boys, and fried chicken, with friendly service, outdoor seating, and a cozy, family-friendly atmosphere. The menu also appears publicly with salads, boudin items, desserts, by-the-pound meats, and to-go prepared offerings.

              Cube Controls

              Visitors can now fully take over the cube instead of only watching it spin. This makes the homepage feel more premium and more “owned” by the user.

              For desktop, drag horizontally and vertically. For touch, drag the cube with one finger. A gentle auto-spin is available, but it starts off so the visitor has control first.
              Builder

              Build your plate

              The left-side options are now organized into category tabs so visitors can browse the menu the same way they think about ordering: meats, sandwiches, po-boys, salads, sides, boudin items, desserts, and extras. Added items can also be removed individually from the plate.

              Drag from categories

              Click a tab to swap categories. Drag any item onto the plate. Click the remove icon on the plate to take it back off.
