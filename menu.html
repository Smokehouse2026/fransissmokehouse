Two Worlds, One Place.

A live one-page demo where you can open your camera, capture a real moment, attach it to GPS, and let other people unlock the experience only when they physically visit that place.

CameraUse your device camera directly in-browser.
GPSTag every post to your current real-world location.
UnlockVisitors only see the full post when nearby.

What this demo proves

People can create memorials, museum stories, restaurant reviews, and legacy posts from the camera itself.
Posts are saved on-device for a clean live demo with no backend required.
The app can simulate the core product before native mobile development.
For camera and GPS permissions, open this on HTTPS or localhost. Some mobile browsers block camera access from plain local files.
