This is where it started, why it started, and what keeps the fire going every morning at 4am.

Read On

US Highway 61 has always been a road of stories. Blues musicians walked it north. Travelers follow it along the bluffs of the Mississippi. Locals have driven it their whole lives without ever thinking twice. And somewhere on that road, in St. Francisville, Louisiana — a smokehouse opened its doors and decided it wasn't going anywhere.

That was 2015. The Francis Smokehouse & Specialty Meats wasn't built on a marketing plan or a trend. It was built on a conviction that the best BBQ is the simplest BBQ — real wood, real fire, real time. No gas assist. No shortcuts dressed up as technique. Post oak and hickory, 225 degrees, and a pit that gets loaded before most people set their alarms.

The Pit
🔥
Every Morning
The fire starts at 4am.
Before the sun comes up, before the highway gets busy, before anyone else in St. Francisville has had their first coffee — the pit is loaded. The wood is set. The temperature climbs to 225°F and it stays there. It doesn't leave until the meat decides it's ready.
Post Oak Hickory 225°F 12+ Hours No Gas

The brisket gets 12+ hrs minimum. The ribs are rubbed and left to develop a bark that cracks when you pull them apart. The pulled pork shoulder smokes low and slow until the collagen breaks down completely and the meat separates with nothing more than a look. This isn't impatience dressed up as efficiency. It's the actual way.

When the smoker is running, you'll smell us before you see the sign. That's not an accident. That's the whole point.

Louisiana Roots
🔥

We're a smokehouse, but we are Louisiana through and through. The deli case runs house-made boudin links, classic boudin balls, pepper jack boudin, crawfish pies, and meat pies — all made from scratch by our team every single day. Because this is St. Francisville. Because this is West Feliciana Parish. And because you can't open a restaurant here and not honor what this place is made of.

The sides are made fresh every morning — corn pudding that guests have driven hours specifically for, baked beans slow-cooked with smoked meat drippings, house-made slaw with a tangy dressing, and sweet potato waffle fries that somehow became their own kind of legend. The daily specials board changes at 6am and features the kind of cooking that reminds you where you are: eggplant pirogue, blackened fish with crawfish tails, Jack Daniel's BBQ shrimp, Creole cream cheesecake.

Why We Do It
🔥

People ask sometimes why we start so early. Why the 4am fire. Why the twelve-hour minimum when you could probably get away with eight. The honest answer is that there's no such thing as getting away with it when the person eating can taste the difference. They always can. They may not know what they're tasting — they may just call it good — but they know.

We're not trying to be the biggest smokehouse in Louisiana. We're not trying to franchise or scale or optimize. We're trying to be the place on US Highway 61 in St. Francisville that makes people slow down, pull over, and eat something that was worth the wait. That's the whole story. That's always been the whole story.

Real food. Real people. Real fire.

— The Francis Smokehouse, since 2015
